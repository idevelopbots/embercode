⚡ CT DMV Quick-Guide

TL;DR ⚡ Quick Start Summary

🎯 Goal: Get your CT Class D Driver's License.
  • Must be 16+ years old to apply.
  • Pass a Vision Test + 25-Question Test (Need 20 correct).
  • Hold permit for 120 days (if taking driving school) or 180 days (if home trained).

📌 Permit Rules & Restrictions

For 16 & 17-Year-Olds, passenger restrictions strictly apply for safety:

🚨 Who can ride with you while learning?
  • A licensed Driving Instructor.
  • OR 1 designated supervisor (20+ years old, licensed 4+ years uninterrupted).

⏱️ Required Hours Breakdown

Every teen driver needs 40 total hours behind the wheel!
  • Classroom Training: 30 Hours total.
  • Parent Class: 2 Mandatory hours for parent/guardian.
  • Safe Driving Class: 8 Hours required for home-trained applicants.

✅ Checklist: Pass Your Road Test

  • [ ] Clear all windows and adjust mirrors before shifting into gear.
  • [ ] Press the foot brake BEFORE shifting out of Park.
  • [ ] Maintain a safe space cushion around your car.
  • [ ] Signal every single turn and lane change.
  • [ ] Bring car to a completely smooth stop without sudden jerks.
