Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Canvas collects only what it needs to work:

  • Server IDs and channel or role IDs tied to your settings.
  • User IDs for features like showcases and galleries.
  • Configuration preferences such as themes, palettes, and embed defaults.

We do not read or store the content of ordinary messages.

2. How We Use Data

Data is used to run commands, remember your server’s settings, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

3. Storage and Security

Data is stored in a secured database with restricted access. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We never sell your data. Information is processed through Discord’s API and our hosting providers solely to operate the Bot, and we may disclose data if legally required.

5. Retention

Server configuration is deleted within 30 days after Canvas is removed from a server. Showcase entries remain until deleted by their author or a server admin.

6. Data Deletion Requests

You can request deletion of your user data, or your server’s data, by contacting us in our support server with the relevant ID. We will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Children

Canvas is intended for users who meet Discord’s minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the date above when we do.
