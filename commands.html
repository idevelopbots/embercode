Commands

Everything Cardinal can do for your community.

/ban

What it does

Permanently removes a member from the server and optionally deletes their recent messages.

Required Permissions: Ban Members
/kick

What it does

Removes a member from the server. They can rejoin with a new invite.

Required Permissions: Kick Members
/mute

What it does

Temporarily times out a member so they can't send messages or join voice channels.

Required Permissions: Moderate Members
/ticket

What it does

Opens a private support ticket channel between a member and your staff team.

Required Permissions: Manage Channels
/setup

What it does

Walks you through configuring log channels, staff roles and ticket categories.

Required Permissions: Administrator
/userinfo

What it does

Shows a member's join date, account age, roles and moderation history.

Required Permissions: View Channel
/purge

What it does

Bulk deletes up to 100 recent messages from the current channel.

Required Permissions: Manage Messages
/warn

What it does

Issues a logged warning to a member and notifies them by direct message.

Required Permissions: Moderate Members
/lockdown

What it does

Locks a channel so only staff can send messages until you unlock it.

Required Permissions: Manage Channels
