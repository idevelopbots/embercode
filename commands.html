Waypoint — Commands

/help

Shows all commands and information about Waypoint.

/setup

Guides you through setting up Waypoint in your server.

/waypoint create

Create a new waypoint for users to navigate to.

/waypoint list

Displays all waypoints saved in your server.

/waypoint delete

Remove a waypoint from your server.

/ping

Check bot latency and responsiveness.
